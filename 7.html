About Mubuga Technical Secondary School (TSS)

Mubuga Technical Secondary School (TSS) is a prestigious educational institution located in Mubuga Sector, Karongi District, Rwanda. Renowned for its commitment to academic excellence and technological innovation, Mubuga TSS provides students with a robust foundation in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills.

Our curriculum emphasizes software development and electrical technology, preparing students to excel in an increasingly digital and technology-driven world.
