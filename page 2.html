Drones are modern flying machines that can be controlled remotely or fly automatically using software. They are becoming very important in today’s world because they help people in many different fields.

  1. Drones are used in agriculture to monitor crops and spray fertilizers.
  2. They help in disaster management by searching for missing people and delivering emergency supplies.
  3. Drones are used in photography and videography to capture beautiful aerial views.
  4. They support military and security forces in surveillance and border monitoring.
  5. Drones help in delivery services by transporting small packages quickly.
  6. They are used in construction to survey land and inspect buildings.
  7. Drones assist scientists in wildlife monitoring and environmental research.
  8. They are useful in traffic monitoring and smart city management.
